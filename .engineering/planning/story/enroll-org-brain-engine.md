## Consumer failure and requested outcome
The org-brain engine's Taskfile brand-check can exit successfully without checking anything when a sibling Atlas checkout is absent. The independently installed b10x-gates 0.1.1 refuses this engine with `repository is not enrolled`. The engine needs an admitted standalone common-check boundary over its exact successor history, without an Atlas checkout or candidate-owned privacy policy.

## Owner action
Gates administrators and delivery owners should review the public engine successor baseline, enroll beyond10x/org-brain in protected policy and signer grants, qualify the published CLI, install coordinated hooks, and supply the immutable reusable workflow and required-check adoption evidence. Preserve existing guards until reviewed migration succeeds. Do not manufacture a trust baseline from a candidate's claim, copy private policy into source, or clear findings by rewriting evidence.

## Acceptance
A committed engine candidate receives a signed receipt for exact new Git objects; reuse verifies without scanning; missing policy/enrollment and incomplete history fail closed. Mutation coverage catches prohibited changed content and metadata. Required remote checks and exact bot publication are verified. Consumer correctness checks remain separate. No model or customer evidence is needed in this public request.

## Scope and handoff
This is an owner request, linked to story:public-shared-gates. Gates owners retain protected enrollment and shared delivery implementation. The engine session changes only its consumer commands and retains an explicit, mandatory existing fence until adoption is available. No Gates implementation or release is performed here. The single owner request does not require a decomposition panel.
